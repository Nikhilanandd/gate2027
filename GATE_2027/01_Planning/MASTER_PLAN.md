### Question Type Breakdown

| Type | Questions (approx) | Marks Each | Negative Marking |
|------|-------------------|------------|-----------------|
| MCQ (1 mark) | ~20 | 1 | −⅓ |
| MCQ (2 marks) | ~25 | 2 | −⅔ |
| MSQ (1 or 2 marks) | ~10–15 | 1 or 2 | None |
| NAT (1 or 2 marks) | ~10–15 | 1 or 2 | None |

### Strategic Implications
- **MCQ penalty is harsh** — only guess if you can eliminate at least 2 options.
- **MSQ is your scoring engine** — no negative marking means partial knowledge still earns marks.
- **NAT requires numerical accuracy** — practice mental math and unit conversions.
- **GA is the easiest 15 marks** — secure at least 12–13 here. It can make or break your rank.

## Key Rules

### The 5 Immutable Rules

1. **Never Skip Revision.** Learning without revision is digging a well with a hole in the bucket. Every Sunday is revision day. No exceptions.

2. **Maintain an Error Log.** After every mock or practice session, log every mistake with:
   - Question number and topic
   - Why you got it wrong (concept gap / silly mistake / time pressure)
   - Correct approach
   - Frequency of this error type

3. **Take At Least 1 Mock Per Week from November.** The mock-analyze-revise cycle is the single most effective preparation method in the last 3 months.

4. **PYQs Are Your Bible.** GATE repeats patterns. Solve PYQs topic-wise during Phase 1–2, then full paper mocks in Phase 3–4.

5. **Protect Your Sleep.** 7 hours minimum. Cognitive performance drops sharply below 6 hours of sleep. Studying tired is counterproductive.

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Studying only comfortable subjects (avoidance)
- Watching too many video lectures without solving problems
- Taking mocks without analyzing them
- Ignoring General Aptitude ("I'll manage" is a trap)
- Comparing progress with others — focus on your own plan
- Starting too many resources — stick to 1–2 per subject
