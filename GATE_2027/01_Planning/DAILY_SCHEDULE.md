# GATE 2027 — Daily Schedule

## Principles

1. **Consistency > Intensity.** 4 focused hours beat 10 distracted hours.
2. **Peak hours for hard subjects.** Never waste morning energy on easy tasks.
3. **Revise before you learn.** Start every session with 15–20 min of previous day's revision.
4. **Pomodoro works.** 50 min study + 10 min break. Maximum 4–5 cycles in a row.
5. **Energy management is time management.** Protect your best hours ruthlessly.

---

## Pomodoro Block Structure

| Element | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Focus Block | 50 min | Deep work — no phone, no distractions |
| Short Break | 10 min | Walk, stretch, water, eyes closed |
| Long Break | 30 min | After 4 blocks — eat, rest, light walk |

> **Tip:** Use a physical timer or Forest app. Phone notifications kill focus.

## Schedule Variant 1: College Day (2–3 Hours)

*Assume college 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Available: 6:00–7:00 AM, 6:00–9:00 PM.*

### Early Morning (5:45 – 7:00 AM) — Revision Block

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 5:45 – 6:00 | Wake up, freshen up, light tea/coffee |
| 6:00 – 6:20 | Revise yesterday's notes (flashcards or formula sheet) |
| 6:20 – 6:50 | Solve 5–8 PYQs from yesterday's topic |
| 6:50 – 7:00 | Log errors, note down doubts |
| 7:00 – 7:30 | Get ready, breakfast, leave for college |

> **Output:** 15–20 min revision + 30 min problem solving = small but consistent daily touchpoint.

### Evening (6:00 – 9:00 PM) — Study Block

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:00 – 6:15 | Decompress from college — snack, change, settle |
| 6:15 – 7:05 | **Block 1:** New topic study (Video lecture or reading) |
| 7:05 – 7:15 | Break — walk around, hydrate |
| 7:15 – 8:05 | **Block 2:** Problem practice on today's topic |
| 8:05 – 8:15 | Break |
| 8:15 – 9:00 | **Block 3:** Mixed practice — PYQs from different topics |
| 9:00 – 9:15 | Wrap up — update error log, plan tomorrow |

### Post-9 PM
- **Do NOT study after 9 PM on college days.** You need 7+ hours of sleep.
- Use this time for dinner, relaxation, light reading, or casual revision (watching a CS video).

### Daily Targets (College Day)
- 1 topic studied or revised
- 10–15 PYQs solved
- Error log updated
- 15–20 min revision of previous material

## Energy Management Tips

### High-Energy Hours (Protect These)
- **6:00 – 10:00 AM** is peak cognitive time for most people.
- Use this for: New concepts, difficult proofs, algorithm design, heavy problem solving.
- **Never waste these hours on:** Revision, watching videos, or easy problems.

### Medium-Energy Hours
- **10:00 AM – 12:00 PM** and **2:00 – 5:00 PM**
- Good for: Practice problems, PYQ solving, reading.
- Moderate focus tasks that require effort but not peak creativity.

### Low-Energy Hours
- **After 5:00 PM** on study days
- Good for: Revision, flashcards, formula review, light reading.
- **Never start new difficult topics** in low-energy slots.

### Energy Boosters (During Breaks)
- **2-min jumping jacks** — instant alertness
- **Cold water on face** — resets focus
- **10-min power nap** (20 min max, set alarm)
- **Short walk outside** — better than scrolling phone
- **Deep breathing** — 4-7-8 technique (inhale 4 sec, hold 7, exhale 8)

### Energy Drainers (Avoid)
- Social media during breaks (scrolling kills focus for 15+ min)
- Heavy meals before study (causes drowsiness)
- Studying in bed (creates sleep association)
- Multi-tasking (switching between subjects mid-block)
- Late-night cramming (destroys next day's productivity)
