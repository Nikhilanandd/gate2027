## 3. Recommended Books (Subject-wise)

| Subject | Primary Book | Alternative/Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Algorithms** | *Introduction to Algorithms* (CLRS) or *Algorithm Design* (Kleinberg-Tardos) | Focus on CLRS for theory; practice from GFG/LeetCode |
| **Operating Systems** | *Operating System Concepts* (Silberschatz/Galvin) | Gate Smashers notes sufficient for GATE-level |
| **DBMS** | *Database System Concepts* (Korth) or *Fundamentals of Database Systems* (Navathe) | Use as reference; GATE questions are conceptual |
| **Computer Networks** | *Computer Networking* (Kurose-Ross) or *Computer Networks* (Tanenbaum) | Kurose-Ross is more exam-friendly |
| **Theory of Computation** | *Introduction to Automata Theory* (Hopcroft-Ullman) or *Introduction to the Theory of Computation* (Sipser) | Sipser is more readable |
| **Compiler Design** | *Compilers: Principles, Techniques & Tools* (Dragon Book — Aho, Lam, Sethi, Ullman) | Heavy book; supplement with YouTube lectures |
| **Digital Logic** | *Digital Design* (Morris Mano) | Direct and to-the-point for GATE |
| **Computer Architecture** | *Computer Organization & Architecture* (Hamacher) or *Stallings* | Hamacher is more commonly referenced |
| **Programming & Data Structures** | *Let Us C* (Yashavant Kanetkar) + GFG practice | Practice implementation on paper |

> **Note:** For GATE, you do NOT need to read entire textbooks. Use them as reference for concepts you don't understand from video lectures. Most toppers rely on video lectures + PYQs more than textbooks.

## 4. Recommended Strategy

### For Budget Students (₹0–2,000)

1. **Concept Building (Month 1–2):** Free YouTube lectures
   - GO Classes for Math + Discrete Math
   - Gate Smashers for DBMS, OS, CN
   - Abdul Bari for Algorithms
   - Neso Academy for COA, Digital Logic, TOC

2. **Practice (Month 2–3):** GateOverflow PYQs (free)
   - Solve all PYQs from 2000–2026 topic-wise
   - Use GateQA for additional practice

3. **Test Series (Month 3–5):** Buy ONE test series
   - **Best value:** GO Classes (₹1,769) — closest to actual exam
   - **Cheapest:** PW (₹1,200) — still good quality

4. **Total cost: ₹1,200–1,769**

### For Comfortable Students (₹3,000–5,000)

1. All of the above
2. **Buy two test series** for cross-practice:
   - GO Classes (₹1,769) + PW (₹1,200) = ₹2,969
   - Or MADE EASY (~₹3,000) for harder mocks

### For Students Needing Full Coaching (₹15,000+)

1. GO Classes or MADE EASY complete course + test series
2. **Only recommended if:** Self-study isn't working, you need deadlines, or you're starting very late
