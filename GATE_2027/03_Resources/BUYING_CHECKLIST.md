## Money-Saving Tips

1. **Don't buy books** — PDF versions and YouTube lectures cover everything for GATE
2. **One test series is enough** — Don't buy 3-4 test series thinking more = better
3. **GateOverflow PYQs are free** — This is the single best resource, and it costs nothing
4. **Wait for discounts** — PW and GO Classes often have sale prices during festivals
5. **Share accounts** — If a friend is also preparing, split the cost of a test series (most allow 1-2 device login)
